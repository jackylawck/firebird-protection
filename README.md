### 📖 About This Project / 關於本專案

**English:**  
This repository is an open-source educational storytelling application created with love by **Dad & Son**. While rooted in personal family learning and bedtime storytelling, it has evolved into a comprehensive **Whole-Person Education (全人教育)** platform. The project bridges foundational character development with forward-looking professional frameworks—ranging from IBDP (TOK, EE, CAS) and STEAM to AI Governance, MIT EPOCH capabilities, and WEF 2025/2030 future workplace competencies.

**中文 (繁體):**  
本專案是由 **父子 (Dad & Son)** 共同研發的開源互動教育故事應用程式。從最初溫馨的家庭親子共讀出發，逐步昇華為一套完整的**「全人教育 (Whole-Person Education)」**互動學習平台。系統將基礎品德教育與前瞻國際框架深度結合——跨越 IBDP 國際文憑核心（TOK、EE、CAS）、STEAM 跨學科探索、AI 人工智能管治、麻省理工 EPOCH 未來能力，以及世界經濟論壇 (WEF) 未來關鍵技能。

### 🌟 Key Pedagogical Pillars / 核心教育支柱

1. **🏛️ PERCCI Character Framework (PERCCI 品格培育)**
   - **P**erseverance (堅毅)、**E**mpathy (同理心)、**R**espect (尊重)、**C**ourage (勇氣)、**C**reativity (創意)、**I**ntegrity (誠信).
2. **🎓 IBDP Magic Academy Framework (國際文憑體系)**
   - Fully visualizes Theory of Knowledge (TOK), Extended Essay (EE), Creativity-Activity-Service (CAS), 6 Subject Groups (HL vs SL), and IA/EA dual assessments.
3. **🚀 Future-Ready Capabilities & AI Literacy (前瞻未來素養)**
   - Integrates **MIT Sloan's EPOCH** framework (Empathy, Presence, Opinion/Ethics, Creativity, Hope/Leadership).
   - Embedded with **NVIDIA Jensen Huang's Generosity Philosophy** & **AI Godfathers' (Bengio, Hinton, LeCun) Human-Centric Wisdom**.
   - Built around the **12 Unicorn Habits** & **WEF 2025/2030 Top 10 Future Skills**.

### 💻 Technical Architecture & Privacy by Design / 技術與隱私架構

- **📱 PWA & Local-First:** Designed as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) with full offline-first capabilities.
- **🛡️ Client-Side Only & Zero Tracking:** Operates purely in the client's browser with zero external telemetry, zero backend data retention, and no tracking scripts.
- **⚖️ Deterministic Pedagogical Engine:** Powered by a deterministic state-machine workflow, ensuring safe, explainable, and age-appropriate learning trajectories.
